The 1996 issues prohibit reproduction of published articles, photographs and drawings without authorization from the administration and editorial team. Both later issues prohibit reproduction without authorization from the editorial team. All four carry a copyright notice naming Nos Oiseaux for their publication year. No Creative Commons or other public text-reuse grant was found in the checked issues.

The archive's public availability is not a grant to redistribute the extracted species accounts. The 100 accounts in `raw/reports/paper_text.csv` contain substantial reproduced prose. For the intended public dataset release, treat their permissions as uncleared and omit this text from public release artifacts until written authorization covers the actual use. Merely excluding the text from the dataset's CC BY licence does not itself authorize its redistribution.

The request should identify the four papers and the 24, 34, 25 and 17 extracted accounts, and seek explicit permission for public redistribution in CSV and website displays, including OCR correction and preservation of paragraph structure. Identify GitHub and Zenodo as distribution channels. If CC BY 4.0 is desired for the text, request that expressly; permission to reproduce is not necessarily permission to offer downstream reuse under CC BY. Ask Nos Oiseaux to confirm its authority to authorize this use and whether author approval is also needed, and preserve its response and attribution conditions.
